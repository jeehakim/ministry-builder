# Competitors and Pricing Benchmarks

**Project:** Shofar Ministry landing-page platform for non-profits (see `PROPOSAL.md`)
**Date:** 2026-11-02
**Method:** Web search (standard mode, extended for Nucleus). I could not open vendor pricing pages directly, so almost every price below comes from search snippets and third-party aggregators. **Confidence column:** `V` = a vendor's own page was in the results; `A` = aggregators only, treat as approximate; `?` = sources conflict. Re-verify anything you quote externally.

---

## 0. Bottom line

- The crowded middle (Wix, Squarespace, Durable, Hostinger, Framer) costs **about $120 to $230 per year** for one custom-domain site. None of them is non-profit-specific, none hands the user an owned, portable site, and the AI is billed by credits.
- The cheap end (Carrd, **$19 to $49 per year**) has no AI. The church-specific end (Nucleus, Tithe.ly, Clover, Ministry Designs) costs **$25 to $250 per month** because it bundles giving, members and apps.
- Nobody found sells "AI draft + non-profit sections + a site that lives in your own account" at **$79 to $99 per year**. That is the gap.
- **Recommended band: $79 to $99 per year per domain, monthly $9 to $12.** Reasoning in section 5.

**Read-across for Group 1**

- **Variations:** I found no source that confirms any of these returns 2 to 3 side-by-side drafts. The "pick one of three" step is therefore a plausible differentiator, but treat "nobody does it" as unverified. Check Durable, B12 and Wix Harmony hands-on before claiming it.
- **Ownership:** Wix, Squarespace, Framer and (per most sources) Durable keep the site on their platform. Lovable (Pro), Relume and Hostinger give some form of code or static export. Nobody found puts the site in the user's own hosting account.
- **AI limits:** The trend is **metered credits** (Framer, Durable, Lovable, B12, Hostinger). Wix bundles AI into the plan. A flat "AI edits included" is rare, which supports a clear per-day quota in the paid tier.

**Read-across for Group 2**

- **Church-specific builders sell a bundle** (site + giving + app + members) and price it at **$25 to $250 per month**. A tiny church or ministry that only needs a clear page does not want to pay $1,200 per year for it.
- **The free donation platforms (Zeffy, Givebutter) are complements, not competitors.** They want a "Donate" link target, which the proposal already uses (`PROPOSAL.md` section 4). Partnering or linking costs nothing and removes any need to handle payments.
- **Setup fees are normal in the niche** ($149 Tithe.ly, $500 to $1,000 Clover, build fees at Ministry Designs). That is evidence people pay for done-for-you.
- **Bilingual:** I found no church or non-profit builder that advertises Korean/English toggling. Korean-hosted general builders (e.g. Dotname, ~KRW 2,500 to 5,000 per month) exist but are not church-specific. Church360° Unite is English-only.

## 3. Pricing-model benchmarks

### 3.1 One-page site, cost per year (US$, annual billing, custom domain)

| Tier | Example | Per year | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ultra-cheap, no AI | Carrd Standard | **$19** (Pro Plus $49) | Billed yearly only; no AI |
| Cheap general builders | Mixo $6 to $9 per month; Framer Basic ~$10 per month; 10Web ~$10 per month | **$70 to $120** | Intro or annual-prepay pricing; Hostinger can be far lower on 48-month terms |

### 3.2 Non-profit discounts

| Program | Discount | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Wix via TechSoup | **70% off** a 2-year Premium plan + free 1-year domain | TechSoup member; one coupon per 2 years; plan tier varies by country |
| Canva | Pro **free** (up to 50 users) | Registered charity; discretionary approval |
| Google Workspace for Nonprofits | Free | Verified non-profit; Sites custom-domain coverage unconfirmed |
| Squarespace | ~10% first payment, code only | Older official page; may be stale |
| Zeffy / Givebutter | Free donation tools | Funded by optional donor tips |
| SITE123 | 40% off paid plans (one review) | Unverified |

**Takeaway:** the strongest existing benchmark is Wix at 70% off. A 2-year Core plan at $29 per month becomes roughly **$8.70 per month, about $105 per year**, but only for TechSoup members and once per two years. Matching that effective price (about $7 to $8 per month) without paperwork is a clear pitch.

### 3.3 Annual vs monthly norms

- Website-builder annual prepay discounts typically run **10 to 30%**; SaaS average ~16.7% (Recurly, cited secondhand). Squarespace monthly is ~30% above annual ($25 vs $19, $39 vs $29); Wix ~30% ($24 vs $17); Framer ~33% ($15 vs $10); Durable ~20%.
- Carrd is **annual-only** at $9 to $49, and still the best-known cheap builder, which shows very small buyers accept a once-a-year charge when the number is small.
- At a 20% discount, annual only pays back after ~10 months of use.
- **Gap:** I found **no data** on how many small non-profits choose annual vs monthly. Church software sites mostly quote monthly but cite annual totals. Cash flow is the likely reason: many small non-profits pay once per budget year, by check or board card, and a $10 per month charge can lapse on card expiry.
- **Website cost surveys** are vendor-written, not independent. Examples: small churches $0 to $500 first year for generic builders; $500 to $2,900 first year for church builders (monthly $39 to $175); some report ~$100 per year with two domains.

## 4. Where this product can win

1. **Non-profit sections out of the box.** Donate (link-out to Zeffy, Givebutter, PayPal), events or service times, programs, team, stories, contact, in a schema the AI fills. General builders make the user assemble these; church builders bury them in a $99+ bundle.
2. **Bilingual (Korean/English) from one document.** No church or non-profit builder found advertises this. It is cheap for a structured-content approach and directly relevant to the Shofar audience.
3. **The user owns the site.** Wix, Squarespace and Framer lock content in; Durable is disputed; Canva and Google Sites are hosted-only. "Your own Cloudflare account, take it with you" is unique among the products reviewed, and it matters for organizations that churn volunteers and webmasters.
4. **Price transparency.** One flat annual number, domain-inclusive or clearly separated, no credit meter, no first-year-only promo. Durable, Hostinger and Squarespace all show a first-term vs renewal gap.
5. **Done-for-you at a small-org price.** Church market setup fees are $149 to $1,000; Ministry Designs management is $247 per month. A one-time **$149 to $299** "we build it, you own it" tier sits far under that.
6. **Free-tier trust.** Zeffy and Givebutter set the expectation that non-profit tools are free or tip-funded. A genuinely free export-and-claim tier fits that expectation and funnels to paid hosting.
7. **Not a bundle.** Church platforms push giving, members and apps. A page-only product at a page-only price has no direct competitor in that niche.

**Where we are weaker:** no ready giving, no members or ChMS, no email tools, no brand recognition in the church market. Wix's 70% TechSoup discount is hard to beat for orgs already in TechSoup. Honest positioning: "your first website", not "your church management system".

## 5. Recommended price band

| Plan | Recommendation | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Free (export or claim to own Cloudflare account) | **$0** | Matches Zeffy and Givebutter norms; Canva/Google already free for eligible orgs; our marginal cost is near zero |
| Hosted (SSL, domain, AI edits, forms, analytics) | **$79 to $99 per year per domain**; show as "about $7 to $8 per month" | Above Carrd ($19 to $49), below Wix and Squarespace sticker ($204 to $228) and below Wix's non-profit effective price (~$105); inside the $70 to $120 cheap-builder cluster |
| Monthly option | **$9 to $12 per month** | Keeps the proposal's $10 anchor but is ~25 to 35% above annual, in line with Squarespace, Wix and Framer gaps; nudges to annual without hiding the monthly price |
| Domain | Pass through at cost (~$11 per year for `.org`), or bundle in the $99 tier | Competitors treat the domain as an add-on; bundling is a simple selling point |
| Done-for-you | **$149 to $299 one-time**, then the hosted plan | Below Tithe.ly ($149) to Clover ($500 to $1,000) setup fees and agency builds ($800+) |

**Why this band**

- **Floor (about $79):** cheap general builders start near $70 to $120 per year, and below that our own support time and payment fees (card fees on a $79 charge are about $2.50) start to bite. Carrd's $19 to $49 has no AI, forms-light and no hosting extras, so it is not the right anchor for a product with AI edits, forms and analytics; pricing near it would undercut us for no gain.
- **Ceiling (about $99 to $120):** Wix with the non-profit discount lands near $105 per year, and Framer Basic is $120. Above that we lose the "cheapest real option for small non-profits" story, and the buyers in the brief have **under $20 per month** total.
- **Per-domain** matches the proposal and Durable's per-site billing; avoid per-seat.
- **Annual-first** because (a) every major vendor discounts annual 20 to 35%, (b) small non-profits budget annually, (c) it halves payment-failure churn. Offer a 30-day refund like Hostinger.
- **Avoid** a credit meter. Use a visible daily AI cap (the proposal's 30 actions per day) so the price stays one number.

**Open questions to settle before pricing goes public**

1. Re-verify the vendor pages for Durable, Squarespace, Wix and Nucleus (see caveats above).
2. Test Durable, B12 and Wix Harmony hands-on for variations and export.
3. Ask 5 to 10 target orgs: monthly or annual? Do they already have a domain? Do they use TechSoup?
4. Confirm whether Stripe/PayPal fees and sales tax change the net on a $79 plan.
5. Decide whether a non-profit proof step (EIN or 501(c)(3)) is needed. The price band does not require it, but a "verified non-profit" price could be lower.
